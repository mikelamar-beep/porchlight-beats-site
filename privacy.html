Privacy Policy – Porchlight Beats Uploader

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Porchlight Beats Uploader ("the app") is a private automation tool operated by the owner of the Porchlight Beats YouTube channel. Its only purpose is to upload videos the owner has created and approved to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Who uses the app

The app is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, the app uses the YouTube API scopes needed to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store any data about viewers or other YouTube users.

Storage and sharing

An OAuth refresh token for the owner's channel is stored encrypted as a secret in the owner's private GitHub repository and is used only to perform uploads. No personal data is sold, shared or transferred to third parties.

Google and YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.
